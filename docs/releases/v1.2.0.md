## Changes since v1.0.0

- Both XD presets now replace tutorial Teddiursa and Hordel's Shadow Togepi.
  The Togepi replacement comes from an accessible two-stage family, and the
  trade accepts it after purification before or after evolution.
- XD's nine regular Poké Spot slots draw from the full supported Pokédex,
  respecting filters and similar-strength matching. Duking's three requests,
  checks and localized dialogue follow the rare slots. Levels and encounter
  rates remain native.
- An optional XD Shadow shiny unlock applies the chosen rate to all 83 Shadow
  IDs. Both presets enable it at 5%. The seed fixes each status; capture and
  purification retain it. The preview uses the same decisions and portraits.
- The README includes advanced-setting screens and genuine gameplay examples.
  The user guide, compatibility notes and validation reports describe the
  current defaults and the differences between the two games.

Colosseum and XD share the same balance settings for their common options.
Pokémon-only keeps native moves, TMs and ordinary items while retaining
accessible evolutions, evolution stones, family diversity and seeded shinies.
Opponent levels, battle EXP, catch rates and purification speed stay native
in both presets.

## Saves and compatibility

Original ROMs and personal saves are preserved. Start a new game to match all
previewed encounters: Pokémon and Shadow PIDs already generated in a save are
not rerolled. Imported profiles retain their saved choices, including older
Shadow locks and Poké Spot pools. Select a current preset to adopt the new
defaults, or enable the individual advanced options.

Wild Poké Spot shinies still use native odds. Shadow Lugia retains its species
and special dark appearance until purification. The tutorial, Togepi, full-pool
and Shadow shiny options can each be disabled separately.

## Verification scope

Checks include automated tests on Windows and Linux, 204 packaged UI states
across six languages, the Rust/Python codec comparison, archive and installer
checks, three-region DOL guards, and preview/output checks against original ROMs.

Controlled native Dolphin probes checked Shadow PID generation at 0%, 5% and
100%, ownership, capture metadata and the purification state transition. These
function-level probes are distinct from a newly played full
capture → save/reload → purification sequence for the new unlock. A full
campaign across every seed and region and a second physical Windows PC are
outside this release's validation scope.

See [validation](https://github.com/KadenHD/pokemon-orre-randomizer/blob/v1.2.0/docs/VALIDATION.md)
and the [changelog](https://github.com/KadenHD/pokemon-orre-randomizer/blob/v1.2.0/CHANGELOG.md).
