# Pokémon Orre Randomizer v1.2.15

XD’s gift preview and journal now show the Pokémon Duking asks for, its
Poké Spot and encounter frequency, and the Pokémon offered in return.
The gift preview aligns the original and randomized names and trade details
across both columns, including shiny entries.
The Simple settings distinguish a species’ share of encounters from the
chance that a newly generated wild Pokémon is shiny.

The preview and journal changes work with the existing generation rules and
the same seed. Colosseum retains its original prerecorded opening movie.

Desktop diagnostics and isolated Dolphin test runs use muted audio.
