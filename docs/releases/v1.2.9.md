# Pokémon Orre Randomizer v1.2.9

- Colosseum's Pyrite rescue and handover use the model, cry and localized
  dialogue of the Pokémon that replaces Plusle. The gift keeps its original
  level and story requirements.
- The Plusle gift pool excludes Pokémon listed at 3.5 m or taller and Snorlax,
  which obscured part of the scene in testing. This filter is specific to
  Plusle and applies with or without evolution-family diversity.
- The Battle Mode Mt. Battle presentation uses the model, cry and localized
  dialogue of the Pokémon that replaces Ho-Oh.

The scenes keep their original camera and show standard colors. A shiny gift
keeps its shiny status once received. Profiles and creation reports from
previous versions remain importable. Regenerating an earlier seed can produce
a different gift or change a family assignment.

See the [story scene audit](../reverse-engineering/STORY_GIFT_SCENES.md) for
the scope of the Dolphin and regional checks.
