# Pokémon Orre Randomizer v1.2.7

- Pokémon sheets show the original ability and move descriptions in both games.
  Descriptions follow the interface language: English, French, German, Spanish,
  Italian or Japanese.
- Move type badges are centered beside the name and description. Shadow moves
  have a localized purple badge with white text in Colosseum and larger black
  text in XD.
- Colosseum Shadow Pokémon sheets show Shadow Rush separately from the regular
  moves. Move names restore missing accents where they match the localized spelling.

See the [user guide](../USER_GUIDE.md#seeds-previews-and-profiles) and
[validation scope](../VALIDATION.md).
