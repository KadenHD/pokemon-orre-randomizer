# Pokémon Orre Randomizer v1.2.19

Trainer dialogue follows more of the teams you face in Colosseum and XD.
Lola's lines in Pyrite account for her team's dance moves. Trainers at Mt. Battle
and Orre Colosseum adjust remarks about their Pokémon, abilities and tactics.

XD's Battle Simulator uses original species data for its fixed rental Pokémon.
The 12 lessons and the Battle CDs keep their original mechanics when you
randomize types, abilities or base stats in the adventure.

In XD, the Gateon Port Spinda scene uses the saved Pokémon's spot pattern and
shiny palette when that encounter is already in progress.

The dialogue and simulator data were checked on the European, American and
Japanese editions, across all six playable languages. Selected scenes were
replayed in muted Dolphin on isolated copies of save files. See the
[validation notes](../VALIDATION.md) for the tested scenarios.

Download the Windows setup EXE to install the app, or use the Windows ZIP to
run it as a portable app.
