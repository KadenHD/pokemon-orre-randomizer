# Pokémon Orre Randomizer v1.2.14

In XD, **Advanced → Poké Spots** has an optional wild shiny rate. It applies
to new ordinary encounters at Rock, Oasis and Cave. The Pokémon keeps the
same shiny result on the field, in battle and after capture. The original odds
remain the default and are retained when importing older profiles.

In Colosseum, the boy imitating Justy in Phenac cheers for the corresponding
member of Justy's randomized team. The sidecar placement has also been adjusted
for short starters. The prerecorded opening movie retains the original footage.

Generate a new ROM to apply the changes. Existing Pokémon and pending Poké Spot
encounters retain their stored personality values.

See the [scene audit](../reverse-engineering/STORY_GIFT_SCENES.md) and the
[shiny rules](../SHINIES.md) for the technical scope.
