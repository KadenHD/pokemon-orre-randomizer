# Pokémon Orre Randomizer v1.2.4

## Music and Home

- Home plays the Pyrite Town theme from Pokémon Colosseum.
- Colosseum and XD each play their own title theme in their workspace.
- All three tracks are included for offline playback. The **Music** switch
  remembers one preference across screens. The **Sounds** switch controls menu
  effects separately.
- Returning to Home from either game leaves both game cards neutral. The border
  highlights on hover or when you reach a card with Tab.
