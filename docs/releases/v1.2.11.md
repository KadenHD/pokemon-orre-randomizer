# Pokémon Orre Randomizer v1.2.11

- Trainer dialogue and Shadow Pokémon reports follow the randomized teams in
  both games. This includes the first battle in Phenac, the Phenac Gym,
  rematches and Mt. Battle opponents.
- Remarks about type themes, moves and abilities adapt to randomized battles.
- XD's lab dialogue, tutorial rematch and final Shadow Pokémon references use
  the species from the seed. The sailor in Gateon Port accounts for a starter
  other than Eevee when offering an evolution item.
- Rewritten lines use the names in the selected game language and fit the
  original dialogue layout without extra forced line breaks.

Generate a new ROM with the same seed and settings to apply these changes.
See the [validation notes](../VALIDATION.md#dialogue-that-follows-a-randomized-species)
for the regional checks and Dolphin replays.
