# Pokémon Orre Randomizer v1.2.13

In XD, the Pokémon at the Rock, Oasis and Cave Poké Spots now match the
encounter in battle. Their shiny odds remain those of the original game.
Several trainer scenes in Gateon Port, on the S.S. Libra and at Cipher Key
Lair also follow the randomized team.

In Colosseum, the motorcycle arrival at Outskirt Stand shows the chosen
starters. Its palettes reflect seeded shiny decisions and a guaranteed
100% rate. When the game rolls shininess during play, the arrival uses
the regular palette. The prerecorded opening movie uses the original
footage. Eagun's partner, Miror B.'s dancers, Duking's gift and the
Mt. Battle reward also reflect the randomized Pokémon.

Generate a new ROM to apply the changes. For more detail, see the
[story scene notes](../reverse-engineering/STORY_GIFT_SCENES.md) and the
[shiny rules](../SHINIES.md).
